# Crown Drake Tech Demo

## How To Play

- Extract the whole ZIP. Open CrownDrakeBeta and double-click Play Crown Drake.bat.
- Complete the Ember Clam tutorial and light the campfire. Press E at the gate beside the tent to hunt the Crown Drake.

## Needs

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and OpenGL 4.3.

## Default Controls

- Mouse to look. WASD to move. Space to jump. Tab toggles running (on by default).
- Left click or P to fire the equipped spell. Q toggles Prescience to show attack areas.
- F toggles third person. Left Ctrl slides. Hold right click to send the blink orb, then release to teleport to it.
- E to interact, claim the downed drake, or mount. Esc opens options and key bindings.
- Insert to quick reset. M to mute. F3 for camp, F4 for the hunt, F9 for the practice range.

## While Riding

- Space to launch. V to land. X switches flight controls.
- In aerobatic flight, W/S pitch and A/D roll.
- Hold right click or Left Alt to fire the Blood Laser.
